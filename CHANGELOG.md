# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

First release.

- `geo-optimize`: TL;DR callout, FAQ section and FAQPage JSON-LD, in HTML or Markdown.
- `seo-gate`: /100 pre-publish gate with a fix-and-rescore loop (target 85, max 3 passes), internal links from the site's sitemap and an anti-AI-tics voice check in English and Spanish.
- `seo-audit`: read-only technical SEO + GEO audit (sitemap, meta, JSON-LD, H1, alt text, robots.txt per crawler, llms.txt; PageSpeed with your own key).
- `content-brief`: SERP-based brief saved to `briefs/<slug>.md`.
- `PRIVACY.md`: the plugin collects no data.
