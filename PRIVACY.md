# Privacy policy: Maquinable GEO plugin

Last updated: 2026-09-24

Maquinable GEO is a Claude Code plugin made of four skills (instructions for Claude). It has no server, no MCP connector, no analytics and no telemetry.

## What data the plugin collects

None. Maquinable does not receive, store or process any data from people who use the plugin: not your prompts, not your articles, not the sites you audit, not your usage.

## What happens when you use it

- The skills run inside your Claude session and use Claude's built-in tools: web search, web fetch and bash. Those tools are provided and governed by Anthropic under your agreement with them.
- The plugin fetches only the pages you ask it to work on (for example, the sitemap, robots.txt, llms.txt and pages of a domain you want to audit), plus search results for the keywords you give it. It sends those sites the ordinary requests a browser or crawler would.
- Files it creates (audit reports, briefs, optimized articles) are saved in your own working directory.

## Optional third-party services

If you choose to configure your own API key for PageSpeed Insights, SerpAPI or DataForSEO, the skills call those services directly with your key and the URLs or keywords involved. Your use of them is governed by those providers' own terms and privacy policies. Maquinable never sees your keys or the results.

## Links to Maquinable

The README mentions Maquinable's paid diagnosis and links to maquinable.com. Following that link is optional. If you submit the form on maquinable.com, that data is handled under the site's privacy policy: https://maquinable.com/privacy
